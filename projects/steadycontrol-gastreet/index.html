Event design

Client / brand
SteadyControl
Project type
Event design
Year
Jun 10, 2024

Challenge and context

Creation of promotional and exhibition materials for SteadyControl’s participation in GASTREET 2024.

SteadyControl - staff efficiency improvement system

Audience
Owners of business in HoReCa
Business need
Needed a design for place, marketing events, social media, video, printed materials and visual metaphore for entire event.

Design system and solution

SteadyControl needed a cohesive set of visual materials to effectively represent the brand at GASTREET 2024 — one of the key events for the hospitality industry.

The goal was to create a consistent exhibition identity that would attract visitors’ attention, communicate the company’s value proposition, and support brand recognition across different physical touchpoints.

Developed a set of exhibition materials, including branded visual assets and promotional elements, following SteadyControl’s existing brand guidelines.
Created a unified visual system for the company’s presence at the event.
Prepared a complete set of exhibition assets adapted for multiple physical formats.

Improved brand consistency across offline communication channels.
Reduced preparation time for future events by establishing reusable design templates and visual guidelines.
